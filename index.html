      About Chloe

      A marketer's mind.
      A storyteller's eye.

      Dallas born · Houston based

      In today's travel landscape, guests are seeking more than a place to stay — they are searching for experiences that feel authentic, elevated, and worth sharing. Chloe K. Lacy is a travel and lifestyle content creator who helps hotels, resorts, spas, restaurants, tourism organizations, and premium travel brands showcase those moments through engaging, high-quality user-generated content.

      Specializing in hotels, boutique properties, resorts, spas, fine dining, destination experiences, excursions, and travel lifestyle brands, Chloe creates content that allows potential guests to envision themselves immersed in every aspect of a brand — from the welcome experience and accommodations to wellness, dining, curated activities, and the journey itself.

      With a background in marketing and entrepreneurship, Chloe brings a unique perspective to content creation. She understands not only how to produce visually compelling content but also how hospitality brands use content to increase awareness, strengthen guest engagement, and drive bookings.

      As a marketer, Chloe has worked with businesses to develop marketing campaigns, customer acquisition strategies, and brand positioning initiatives. Today she applies that expertise to travel and hospitality content designed to support brand growth and inspire future guests.

      Her style blends luxury, lifestyle, wellness, and storytelling — capturing the details that make a guest experience memorable, and staying aspirational while remaining genuine and relatable.
